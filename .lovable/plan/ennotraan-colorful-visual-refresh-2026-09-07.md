# Ennotraan colorful visual refresh

## Goal
Make the full website feel brighter, more engaging, and more memorable while keeping the white foundation and the new blue logo as the visual anchor.

## What will change
- Expand the brand palette around Ennotraan blue with supporting sky, coral, yellow, mint, and soft lavender accents.
- Replace the restrained page backgrounds with layered, lightweight motion: drifting abstract shapes, orbiting dots, soft line patterns, and scroll-triggered accents that respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Redesign the home opening area around a vivid family habit-building photograph while preserving the current message and calls to action.
- Add tailored photography to the Schools, Colleges, and Corporates pages so each audience is immediately recognizable.
- Give page openings, cards, numbered steps, quote bands, calls to action, and the footer distinct color treatments without losing readability or the clean white base.
- Improve movement across the site with subtle image parallax, staggered reveals, floating accents, and richer hover feedback.
- Keep all existing words, routes, navigation, contact details, and join-form behavior unchanged.

## Technical details
- Add reusable decorative and image-led presentation patterns rather than repeating custom effects page by page.
- Extend semantic color tokens in the global design system; avoid hardcoded colors in page code.
- Use optimized local generated imagery with meaningful alternative text and stable aspect ratios.
- Ensure animations stop or simplify for visitors who prefer reduced motion.
- Verify the home page and every content page at desktop and mobile sizes, including navigation, text contrast, image cropping, and form layout.
